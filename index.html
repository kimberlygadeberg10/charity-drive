Your Next Chapter

Because Your Story Doesn't End Here

Your Next Chapter is a charity-driven event center dedicated to supporting individuals affected by depression, suicide, and traumatic life events. We create safe spaces for healing, life long connections, and hope.

Explore Our Events

Our Mission

Founded by survivors, Your Next Chapter exists to remind individuals that even in the darkest moments, there is a path forward. Through community-driven events, education, and outreach, we help people take meaningful steps towards recovery and healing.

What We Offer

Support groups, healing workshops and exercise, community events

Healing Events

From candlelight vigils to health and wellness workshops, our events are designed to foster understanding, support, hope, healing and education.

Community Support

Your Next Chapter brings together survivors, advocates, volunteers, and the community to build strong, compassionate support networks that will stand the test of time.

Volunteer Opportunities

Our volunteers are the heart and soul of our mission, helping us to create meaningful experiences that change lives.

Join The Movement

Whether you're looking to attend an event, volunteer your time, donate to our cause, or simply learn more, we invite you to be a part of a community where healing begins and hope grows.
