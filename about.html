About Us

Your Next Chapter was founded by survivors who understand pain, loss, and recovery. We exist to provide hope, connection, and healing for individuals impacted by depression, suicide, and traumatic life experiences.

Our Mission

We believe that no one's story ends in their darkest chapter. Through community events, education, and compassionate support. We help individuals take their next step forward.

Our Values

  • Compassion & understanding
  • Community & connection
  • Hope & healing

Our Team & Volunteers

Our team is made up of survivors, advocates, and volunteers who believe in power of compassion, connection, and hope. Each person plays a vital role in helping others take their next step forward.
